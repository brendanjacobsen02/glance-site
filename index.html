How it works

Tap once, get matched with another person waiting, talk over live video — tap Next to move on.

Who's here

Adults 18+, verified by a real phone number. A quick face check keeps the bots and fakes out.

Not recorded

Video and audio go straight phone-to-phone over WebRTC — the server brokers the intro and never sees the call.

Matched, not shuffled

Interests and vibe plus a light read on personality nudge you toward people you'd actually want to talk to.

Easy to leave

Report and block live in the call. Bad actors get auto-banned and reviewed by a human, not a queue.

Self-hosted, no data brokers. The Privacy Policy and Terms, linked below — short, plain, and worth a read.
