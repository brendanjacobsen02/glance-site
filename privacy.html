Privacy Policy

Effective June 2026

Glance is built and run by an individual developer — not a company. This policy is written plainly because the data story is short: a phone number to keep accounts real, a live call that goes phone-to-phone and is never recorded, and a few signals that help match you with someone worth talking to. Questions or requests go to leonzhou@berkeley.edu.

Who runs Glance

Glance is an independent project operated by a single individual developer. There is no company, parent organization, or legal entity behind it. When this policy says "we" or "I," it means that one operator. You can reach the operator at leonzhou@berkeley.edu.

18+ only

Glance is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and accounts are not knowingly created for minors. If you believe a minor is using Glance, email leonzhou@berkeley.edu and the account will be removed.

What Glance collects, and why

Your phone number

Sign-in is a phone number plus a one-time SMS code. The number is verified through Twilio (their Verify service sends the code) and is stored to identify your account, keep duplicates and bots out, and let you sign back in. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Camera and microphone, for live calls

A video call needs your camera and microphone. Glance requests those permissions only when you start a call, and you can revoke them at any time in your device settings. During onboarding a quick face check confirms a real person is present.

Calls are not recorded or stored. Video and audio travel directly between the two people on the call, peer-to-peer over WebRTC. The Glance server brokers the introduction (who connects to whom) but does not see, record, or keep the contents of your call. When two phones can't reach each other directly, encrypted media may be relayed through a TURN relay from metered.ca — it passes the encrypted stream along and does not store it.

Matching signals

To match you with someone you'd actually want to talk to, Glance uses the interests and "vibe" you choose during onboarding, plus a light personality read. During onboarding you write one short free-text answer; that single answer is sent to Anthropic to infer Big-Five personality traits used only for matching. Glance does not use this for advertising or profiling beyond match quality.

Call-activity metadata

Glance keeps basic metadata about call activity — things like when a match happened, how long a session lasted, and skip/Next events — to run matchmaking, fight abuse, and improve matching. This is metadata, not call content; the words, video, and audio of your conversations are never captured.

Safety reports

If you report or block someone, Glance records that report (who, when, and the reason you choose) so it can act on it. Reports may include limited context needed to review the incident, but never a recording of the call. See Terms & Community Rules for how reports are enforced.

Third parties Glance relies on

Glance uses a small set of service providers to function. Each handles only the narrow slice of data described below. There are no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking SDKs.

Twilio
Sends the SMS verification code to your phone number.
Anthropic
Receives your one short free-text onboarding answer to infer personality traits used for matching.
Fly.io
Hosts the self-hosted Glance backend (accounts, matchmaking, signaling).
metered.ca
Provides a TURN relay that forwards encrypted call media when a direct phone-to-phone connection fails. It stores nothing.

No advertising, no tracking

Glance contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking SDKs. Your data is not sold or shared with data brokers. There is no ad profile of you, because there are no ads.

How long data is kept

Account data (your phone number, profile choices, and matching signals) is kept while your account exists. Call-activity metadata and safety reports are kept as long as needed for matchmaking and abuse prevention. Because calls are never recorded, there is no call content to retain or delete.

Deleting your data

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Email leonzhou@berkeley.edu from, or referencing, the phone number on the account. Your account and the data tied to it will be removed, except where limited records must be kept to enforce a ban or comply with the law.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Continuing to use Glance after a change means you accept the updated policy. For anything unclear, email leonzhou@berkeley.edu.
